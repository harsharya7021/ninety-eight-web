02 The room What Retain is, and the eight cases filed under it

The feed is held week after week

The room:

Retain — Social, on the marketing page — is the instrument the studio lives in. Reach you rent stops the day you stop paying; this room builds the part you keep. The channels we make for a brand are held here week after week: the feed, the design system, the shoots.

The feed is above — social creatives, stories and posts for eleven brands. The shoots have a page of their own, because a shoot is a channel we own, not a service sold apart from it. The identity work has moved too: it belongs at the start of the story rather than the foot of this page, so it now sits under Branding, the first instrument.
