01

The short version, in Harsh’s words

Harsh Arya, founder
  1. Before

    Engineering, research at Georgia Tech, and a camera: photography, design, film, dance videos with a friend. A fairly humble start; some of my cousins still drive auto-rickshaws in the village.

  2. 2020

    1 January, midnight. While my friends partied, I went home early with one of them to pitch his father, who ran a nightclub. Esquire Club became the first client, before 98 had a name.

  3. 2020

    I pitched Pratima Singh, who captained India at basketball, and ran her social media. When her husband, Ishant Sharma, left his management agency, she gave me the chance to manage him. I was twenty. Four years earlier I’d been talking about his bowling in a classroom.

  4. 2021

    Friends left for master’s degrees and placements. I rented 250 square feet, stayed its only employee, and kept the lights on with one cloud kitchen’s retainer. I’d made a fool of myself in meetings, so I took a speaking course. Then I started cold calling. It is humbling. You lose all shame.

  5. 2022

    ISB admitted me. I deferred, and the safety net made me take bigger risks. Ishant signed with us exclusively; restaurants came on from Bombay, Bangalore and Delhi NCR. Things have a lagging effect.

  6. 2023

    Fifty-five clients on the roster, and I wasn’t happy: no social life, family and health last. I couldn’t hand 98 to anyone, so I wound it down on purpose and closed the loop before ISB.

  7. 2024

    ISB. The structure, and the science behind what I had been doing by hand.

  8. 2025

    A year off, unlearning the hustle.

  9. 2026

    98 starts again. Sensibly, this time.
