01 The Snapshot

Nao Asian Table · by Berco's. Pan-Asian · modern fusion.

02 The Challenge

Nao is a Berco's spinoff entering the modern pan-Asian space. The parent brand has 30+ years of Indo-Chinese equity — we needed to launch Nao as its own concept, not a Berco's extension.

Brief: separate the Nao brand voice while still leveraging Berco's reach. Land 'Live in the Nao' as a tagline.

The roster: one mega creator (Shreya, 480K) + one Delhi-rooted food voice (Drishty).

03 The Moves

'Live in the Nao' tagline. Every creator caption ended with the same four words — burned into IG search and saved-content for a recall handle.

Mega + local pairing. Shreya carries city-agnostic reach; Drishty roots the brand in Delhi's food map. Two voices, two jobs.

Asset double-duty. Each creator delivered 1 reel + 4–6 stories — content that re-ran on Nao's owned feed for 4 weeks post-campaign.
