02 The Challenge

Noida is rarely the venue side of a creator campaign — most rosters skew Delhi or Gurugram. Local needed two creators willing to travel + make Noida feel like a destination.

Brief: position Local as the weekend bar of choice for the 24-32 set in Noida — not a fallback, but a first pick.

Sound matters here. ROSALÍA was the audio lock — high-energy, late-night, instantly readable as 'going-out content'.

03 The Moves

Weekend-only window. All briefs scheduled to drop Friday morning — by Saturday night, the saved-content was driving real bookings.

Sound-first edits. Every frame cut to the same Latin-pop bed. The story tray became a single audio brand.

Noida-rooted voices. Both creators are Noida-or-near. Their followers don't have to cross an emotional border to come.
