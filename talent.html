From a name to a brand.

Ishant Sharma · the walk, 2022 →

01 · Before us

Already a name. Not yet a brand.

105 Tests, 311 wickets, a thirteen-year India career. What was missing wasn’t fame. It was the mechanism to monetise that name without diluting it.

A talented bowler taking sponsor calls is not a property. It’s an inbox.

01

02 · 2022

The signing.

Exclusive representation, won in 2022 — sole agency-of-record for brand deals, social media and personal communications.

The shift in posture: from what’s the brief to what does the brand sound like, what is it worth, what kind of partner do we want. An ownable property with a managed brand surface.

04 · April 2022

The face of RuPay × IPL.

NPCI’s first-ever IPL campaign — RuPay. Be On-The-Go — with Ishant as the lead face. Five films across TV, OTT, digital and social; the Howzzaaaatt! gag positioning RuPay’s payment speed against his bowling.

Harsha Bhogle and Virender Sehwag amplified on Twitter. DDB Mudra handled the creative. We ran the talent — national scale, the most-watched cricket window of the year, our talent at the centre. Covered by ET BrandEquity.

04

05 · The thesis in action

Rent the channel. Don’t sell the slot.

Instead of selling sponsorship slots one-off, Spinny ran a multi-week placement programme through Ishant’s handles, anchored on archival cricket reels — the format the metrics team had flagged as the year’s highest-performing.

That single format is most of the +14M jump in reels views. For Spinny: a sustained share-of-voice in cricket-fan circles that no single-window IPL slot could buy.

05

06 · Mainstreet × CRED

Sneakers as content.

A streetwear spree at Mainstreet Marketplace, sponsored by CRED, turned into a piece of content: Ishant on the floor, picking a haul, talking sneaker stories on camera. A brand placement that didn’t read as one.

Streetwear is a Gen-Z signal a Test bowler doesn’t usually own. We built the room where he could own it credibly — CRED as the sponsor of the visit, not the subject of the post.

06

07 · Ranveer × Ishant

Two rooms. One bridge.

Cross-talent collaborations between cinema’s biggest livewire and cricket’s quiet veteran — engineered, not coincidental. Joint content windows, cross-posted reels, brand-aligned moments.

The bridge work extended to Sanyam Sharma and Rajesh Yadav — separate creator audiences, the same engineered cross-over. The thesis is the bridge, not the host.

07

08 · The long deals

Business partner, not vendor.

RD Accessories — a homegrown D2C brand that needed structure, not stardom. We built the deal architecture: rights, licensing, content windows, reporting cadence. Then we shipped the campaign.

Asian Business School — the partnership that didn’t run on a quarter. Multi-year campus engagements; content for ABS that reads as content for Ishant, not as paid placement.

08

09 · The daughter announcement

The metric was trust.

We were the ones to break the news — not because we wanted the engagement, because we’d earned the trust to. The post went out on his timeline, in his voice, with no brand handle in the caption.

The documentary window came the same way: a long-form film on cricket, identity and the modern Indian player. Coverage a competitor can’t sponsor their way into.
