About · Harsh Arya

Harsh Arya

Founder · Ninety-Eight

Engineer by training, operator by trade. Built and ran 98 between 2020 and 2023 across sports, hospitality and direct-to-consumer brands — closing exclusive deals with international athletes, taking restaurants from launch to retainer, and growing the book to 55+ active accounts. Paused the practice in 2023 to complete an ISB MBA, and now leads the v2.0 chapter as a creative powerhouse.

Background

Engineering undergrad with research time at Georgia Institute of Technology. MBA from the Indian School of Business. Reads widely across economics, design history and culture; travels often. Brings the equal halves of art and science to client work — equally at home in a brand-launch room and a three-statement model.

Writing & thought leadership

Writes a regular column on Gen Z economics for The Economic Times. Runs Gen Z Economics, a research-led blog in collaboration with Professor Tantri (ISB / Power Finance Corporation). Authored a documentary on influencer culture and the Gen Z creator economy, and a forthcoming playbook for early-career founders.

The name "98"

Three stories, one name. The honest one: a wall, somewhere in Delhi, with "98" painted on it — spotted during a YouTube shoot with a friend in the late 2010s. The shorthand one: 1998, the cusp year — anyone born after is part of the same generational frame. The third stays internal, for clients who ask in person.
